Frequently Asked Questions

Some common questions about our furniture and project services.

Do you take complete furniture contracts?

Yes. Furniture projects can be discussed as complete contract work depending on the scope, material and site requirements.

Do you provide site measurement?

Site measurement can be coordinated for projects where physical measurement is required before quotation.

Do you work outside Kanpur?

INDRA HOME SOLUTIONS is intended to work across Uttar Pradesh. Availability depends on project size, location and execution requirements.

Can I get a quotation before starting?

Yes. A quotation can be prepared after understanding the project requirements. Final pricing may change if measurements, materials or specifications change.

Do you provide only furniture?

Furniture is the priority. Other selected services such as electrical and interior work can also be coordinated according to the project.

How can I contact you?

You can call or send your requirement on WhatsApp using the contact buttons on this website.

Why Choose a Single Project Coordinator?

Instead of managing separate people for every part of a project, customers can communicate their requirement at one point.

We can coordinate measurement, quotation, material discussions, execution teams, installation and basic project follow-up according to the agreed scope.

This model is especially useful for customers who want furniture work done without having to individually search for every worker.
